Color v2 spans **two layers of the design system** and **two groups of people**. Platform owners define and publish VDS. Product teams consume it in Figma and in code.

## Migration: who to involve

**Frontend engineers are the primary audience** for this guide. Read <DocLink slug="gettingStarted">Getting Started</DocLink> and <DocLink slug="tokenMapping">Token Mapping</DocLink>, then apply changes in your team's repos and services.

**Pull in the Verity platform team** when you need help vetting token choices, component patterns, or Storybook references:

- **Vivien** (PM): prioritization, rollout timing, cross-team dependencies
- **Meg Kim** (design): Figma Collection, token gaps, designer sign-off on semantic bindings
- **Melina O'Dell / Verity engineering**: Verity components, production Storybook, implementation questions

**Work with your product designer and PM** on product-specific decisions: which surfaces migrate first, dark mode timing, and UI that does not map cleanly to existing tokens. Platform owners define the system; product teams decide how and when to adopt it in their area.
