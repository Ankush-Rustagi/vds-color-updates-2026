# Who Does What

The Verkada design platform has two parts: **VDS** (Figma) and **Verity** (code). They are not synonymous; the goal is to align them over time. Platform owners publish both; product teams adopt them in Command.

## Product teams (consumers)

Product lines (Cameras, Access Control, Alarms, and others) adopt Color v2 in their surfaces. They do not own the token spec.
