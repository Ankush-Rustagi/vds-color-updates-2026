# Getting Started

Follow these steps to migrate a Command surface from legacy CSS variables to Color v2 semantic tokens.

**Primary audience:** frontend engineers migrating product repos. For platform vs product team roles and who to ask for help, see [Who Does What](/docs/intro-to-color-v2-who-does-what--docs).

## Step 1: Understand the scope

Read [What's Changing](/docs/color-v2-updates-what-s-changing--docs) for token counts and visual deltas your stakeholders should expect.

## Step 2: Map your legacy variables

Open [Token Mapping](/docs/color-v2-migrations-token-mapping--docs) and search for your team's legacy `--color-*`, `--bg-*`, or `--vc-*` prefixes. For anything not listed, use [Semantic Colors](/docs/reference-semantic-colors--docs).

## Step 3: Learn the reference patterns

Work through [Alert Button](/docs/color-v2-migrations-alert-button--docs) and [Checkbox](/docs/color-v2-migrations-checkbox--docs) before migrating other components. These cover destructive actions and form controls.

## Step 4: Designers — rebind in Figma

Follow [Figma Workflow](/docs/color-v2-migrations-figma-workflow--docs) to replace raw hex with semantic variables from Collection.

## Step 5: Ship and validate

Use the PR checklist on each migration page. Compare swatches in Semantic Colors against your implementation.
